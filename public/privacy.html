Privacy notice

Short and plain. Last updated on launch day.

What is collected

  • Star ratingStored
  • Your messageStored, published
  • Display nameOnly if you type one
  • Session typeOnly if you pick one
  • Email, phone, accountNever asked for
  • Your IP addressNot stored — see below

IP addresses

To stop one person flooding the page with fake reviews, a short-lived one-way hash of your connection address is kept for the current hour. It is salted, cannot be reversed into an address, is never shown to anyone, and anything older than 30 days is deleted automatically.

Moderation

Every review is read by the therapist before it is published. Reviews that contain other people's personal details, advertising or abusive language are not published. There is no edit function; if you want a published review removed, contact the therapist and quote the date of your review.

Cookies

No tracking cookies, no analytics, no advertising pixels. A single cookie exists for the therapist's own login session on /admin and is never set for visitors.

Removal requests

Message the therapist on WhatsApp with the approximate date of your review and it will be deleted.
